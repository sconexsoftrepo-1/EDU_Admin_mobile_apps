EDUNEXA COLLEGE
INITIAL DATA COLLECTION

This pack contains 13 separate Excel files. Complete only the files relevant to your assigned department. Each file contains its own Guide with field definitions and fictional examples.

HOW TO ENTER DATA
1. Column headers are in row 6. Enter your first real record in row 7.
2. Fields marked * are required. See the Guide for conditional requirements.
3. Use permanent codes consistently across all files. Reuse one parent code for siblings. Mobile numbers and postal codes must stay as text.
4. Use YYYY-MM-DD dates and the provided dropdown choices. Leave unknown optional details blank; do not replace them with zero or N/A.
5. Amounts are in currency units, normally INR, not paise. For percentages enter 25 for 25 percent.
6. The first 200 entry rows are prepared. Extend the Excel table for more rows to retain formatting and formulas.
7. Do not rename files, tabs or column headings, insert header rows, merge entry cells or type into grey calculated columns.
8. Do not enter passwords or bank login details. Document references may be entered; files will be uploaded separately later.
9. Return completed files through the agreed private transfer channel.

FILES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
01 Students and Parents: admissions team; student profiles, adult contacts and family relationships.
02 Courses and Subjects: academic office; available programs, subjects and semester-wise curriculum.
03 Employees: HR; all employees, including teachers and non-teaching staff.
04 Teachers: academic office; faculty specialization, credentials and teaching responsibilities.
05 Staff: HR or department heads; non-teaching duties, shifts and assignments.
06 Student Enrollments: academic office; course, batch, year, semester, section and roll-number placement.
07 Fee Heads: accounts; academic, tuition, bus, hostel, mess, admission, examination, laboratory, library, deposit and other heads.
08 Fee Structures and Scholarships: accounts; course and installment rates before scholarships, scheme rules and student awards.
09 Student Charges and Payments: accounts; actual head-wise charges, scholarship deductions, received payments and payment allocations.
10 Bus Transport: transport office; routes, drivers, stop rates and student allocations.
11 Hostel Rooms and Allocations: hostel office; rooms, beds, charges and student occupancy periods.
12 Leave Types: HR and academic office; employee and student leave entitlements and approval rules.
13 College Calendar and Events: college administration; holidays, academic dates, exams and events.

COLLECTION ORDER
Confirm campus and department codes with the college administrator before entry. Complete courses, employee records, student and parent profiles, and fee heads first. Follow with enrollments, teacher/staff extensions, fee structures, scholarship awards, bus and hostel details. Enter actual charges and historical payments last. Leave policies and calendar events can be collected independently.

FINANCE EXAMPLE
For a tuition charge of INR 25,000, a non-scholarship discount of INR 1,000, no fine or tax, and an approved scholarship applied of INR 5,000:
Gross before scholarship = INR 24,000.
Net payable after scholarship = INR 19,000.
A settled payment allocation of INR 10,000 leaves INR 9,000 outstanding.
Enter academic, tuition, bus, hostel and other charges as separate rows under their own fee heads. Do not repeat a payment for every fee head; split it using Allocations.

The portal upload and reviewed import will be provided through the future Python backend. These files collect the source information required for that implementation.
